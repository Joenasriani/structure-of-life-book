# The Structure of Life

Canonical source for the study-book sales page.

Production: https://the-structure-of-life.vercel.app

## Target

The page must sell first to readers who want stronger ways to structure problems, ideas, models and systems. It should also be immediately legible to AI builders, product people, researchers and creative technologists as a reusable structural library — without turning the book into an “AI book” or using generic AI language.

The AI signal is indirect and earned through the book itself: state, constraint, memory, feedback, error, change, competing models, hidden variables, transfer between disciplines, and testing the model against the world.

## Sales-page rules

- One-page selling experience on desktop.
- The book must be understandable in roughly one second.
- The primary purchase action must be large, obvious, and visible immediately.
- Keep **SAMPLE READ** visible above the fold and preserve the in-page reader.
- State the offer plainly: **Book + Free AI Frameworks**.
- No jargon unless the book itself requires the term.
- No generic AI copy or generic “future” language.
- No generic AI imagery: no brains, neural nets, glowing nodes, robots, circuitry, holograms, prompt windows, gradient clouds, abstract 3D blobs, or synthetic tech scenery.
- No generic generative UI: no SaaS cards, pills, dashboards, glassmorphism, badge clutter, startup gradients, fake data panels, or decorative interface chrome.
- No common conversion-template sections or familiar visual patterns that could belong to another product.
- Every visible device must come from the book’s actual logic.
- Color is functional, not decorative. Current domain register: **CODE / LIFE / MIND / SYSTEMS**.
- Cover language should imply a reusable reference system through the **Structure Index** and the actual book primitives: state, constraint, memory, feedback, error, change.
- The cover should quietly suggest that these structures can organize models and reasoning systems without ever saying “AI book” on the cover.
- Author credit stays visually secondary: `by J. NASR`.

## Selling lines

> A structure in one discipline. A missing variable in another.

> What one field treats as ordinary may be the missing idea in another.
